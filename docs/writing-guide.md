# Writing guide

Use this for every new essay. Keep the voice plain and concrete.

## Opening

Answer the reader's main question in the first two sentences. State what the piece is about and who it helps. Do not open with a story setup or a soft throat-clear.

## Headings

Prefer question-style H2s when they match how someone would search (`Why does X fail?`, `How do you choose Y?`). Keep H2s short. Do not use an H1 in the body; the page title is the H1.

## Terms

Define a technical term the first time it appears. After that, use the term without re-explaining it. Link out only when the definition needs a fuller source.

## Structure

1. Answer first.
2. Context and constraints.
3. The approach or argument.
4. Trade-offs and failure modes.
5. A short close with one next step.

## Links

In every essay:

- Link to the series hub when the piece belongs to a series.
- Link to two related items (another essay, a topic hub, or a case study) with visible anchor text that names the destination.
- Keep breadcrumbs visible. They must match the BreadcrumbList JSON-LD on the page.

## Trust rules

- Never invent metrics, dates, or client names.
- Use real publish and update dates only.
- No cadence claims you cannot keep.
- No em-dashes or en-dashes in body copy.

## End of essay

Close with one primary call to action: subscribe. Related links come after that, not before.
